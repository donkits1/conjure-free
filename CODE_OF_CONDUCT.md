# Code of conduct

Conjure's issue tracker and pull requests are a working space. A few expectations:

- Be respectful and specific. Critique code and behavior, not people.
- Assume good faith; ask before accusing.
- No harassment, slurs, personal attacks or doxxing, in any channel connected to this project.
- Keep private data out of reports: your own, your employer's and other people's.

The maintainer may edit, lock or remove content and may block people who ignore this. If something happens that you think the maintainer should know about, open an issue titled "Conduct" with as little detail as you are comfortable sharing publicly, or wait for a private contact channel to be announced in the README.
